## Licence implications

- **Ultralytics AGPL-3.0**: acceptable for this portfolio, Term 4 submission, and the Greenlam pilot. Before any commercial sale the detector must be swapped to an Apache-2.0 implementation through the `Detector` adapter, or an Ultralytics Enterprise licence bought.
- **CC BY-NC-SA datasets** (SH17, MVTec, MOT17): non-commercial only. Weights fine-tuned on them inherit the same restriction. The pilot uses ~2 hours of site footage labelled with permission for any production model.
- **Pexels video**: free for commercial and non-commercial use; people in the footage are blurred at ingestion regardless.

## COCO-pretrained class use

The demo runs COCO weights and uses only the `person` class for footfall, queue, checkout, and exclusion rules. Helmet and vest classes appear only when fine-tuned PPE weights are present.
