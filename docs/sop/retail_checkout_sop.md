# Retail checkout and shelf SOP — RAQIB demo store

Standard operating procedure for the floor manager and till supervisors. The watcher (RAQIB) proposes; a person decides. Version 2026-09.

## 1. Opening a third till

Open a third till when either condition holds for more than two minutes: more than three customers waiting in any queue zone, or the M/M/c utilisation ρ shown on the dashboard exceeds 0.85. The till supervisor calls the nearest floater, who logs in within 90 seconds. RAQIB raises a "Propose opening a till" card with ρ and the expected wait; the floor manager approves or rejects it in Actions. A rejected proposal is not raised again for 15 minutes.

## 2. Closing a till

Close a till only when every queue zone has two or fewer customers for ten consecutive minutes and ρ is below 0.6. Never close the last two tills between 17:00 and 21:00 on Thursday, Friday and Saturday.

## 3. Queue escalation

If a queue stays over the limit for more than five minutes after a till is opened, the floor manager opens the express lane and announces it. If it persists past ten minutes, page the store manager. Severity-3 safety events (blocked exit, medical) always page the store manager immediately; no automation may delay or cancel that page.

## 4. Shelf replenishment

A shelf gap alert (rule R11) means the shelf ROI has been more than 40 percent empty for five minutes. The replenishment associate restocks within 20 minutes for dairy (shelf B3) and bread (shelf C2), and within 45 minutes for confectionery (shelf A1). Restock work orders are raised automatically; mark them done in the tracker so on-shelf availability is measured correctly.

## 5. Price tags and planogram

Every Monday before opening, check price tags on the promotional bays against the price list document. A price mismatch is severity 1 and is fixed before 10:00. Planogram drift on a bay is corrected at the next replenishment round.

## 6. Privacy on the floor

Faces are blurred on the edge box before any frame is stored or sent. Nobody at the store can see an unblurred frame. Clips are kept for 30 days, events for 400 days. Staff may request the privacy notice at the customer service desk.

## 7. Weekly review

Every Monday the manager reads the weekly report: service level (target 90 percent of 15-minute slots with a queue of three or fewer), on-shelf availability (target 98 percent), the staffing plan, and the three recommendations. Decisions are recorded in the pilot log.
